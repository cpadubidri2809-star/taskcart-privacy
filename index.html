2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

TaskCart does not require you to create a traditional account with a name, email, or password. You may enter the following information directly into the App:

  • Task descriptions, task types, and recipient names
  • Inventory items, quantities, and prices
  • Transaction history and notes
  • Reminder times and alarm settings
  • App preferences (currency, sound, etc.)

This information is stored locally on your device only. It is not transmitted to us or any third party. See Section 3 below.

3. Local Data Storage

All task, inventory, and transaction data you enter stays on your device using an encrypted SQLite database. It is not uploaded to any server.

You can export backups to your device or your own Google Drive. We do not have access to those backups.

When you uninstall the App, all local data is deleted automatically.

4. How We Use Information

We use the limited data we collect for:

  • App functionality — verifying your Pro subscription through RevenueCat
  • Crash diagnostics — fixing bugs identified by Sentry
  • Security — preventing fraud and abuse of subscription services

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.
